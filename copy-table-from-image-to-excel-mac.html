Copy a table from an image to Excel on a Mac

You have a table in an image and you want it in cells. Here is the quick way if you use Excel, then what Kare adds the rest of the time.

The short answer

If you use Excel for Mac, Insert > Data From Picture does the job. Kare is for the rest of the time: a table in a PDF, a web page, a video call or a screenshot, in any app.

Draw an area, paste

Draw an area around the table, press the shortcut. Paste into Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets: each value lands in its own cell.

When the print is too small

If Kare is not sure about a number, it asks you to check instead of copying a mistake.

PDFs and images you already have

Copy files in the Finder (⌘C): Kare reads their text, scans included.

100% on your Mac

macOS reads the image on your Mac. No account, no data sent.
