Copy text from a video or a screen share on your Mac

A slide in a video, a line in a tutorial, the text of a shared screen: it is on your screen, but you cannot select it. Here is the free way first, then what to do when it is not enough.

The free way first: Live Text

  1. Pause the video (in Photos, QuickTime Player, Safari or most apps that play video).
  2. Click the Live Text button that appears on the paused frame, then select the text.
  3. Copy it with ⌘C.

Live Text only works on a still frame, and not in every app.

When Live Text is not enough

A shared screen in a call, a player that will not pause, text that disappears after a second, a slide in a video conference: Live Text is not offered there.

The Kare way: draw an area, paste

  1. Press ⇧⌘2 and draw an area over the text, wherever it is on your screen.
  2. Kare reads it on your Mac and copies it. A table lands one value per cell when you paste it into Excel or Numbers.
  3. Paste it where you need it.

Kare reads what is on your screen at the moment you draw the area, whatever app displays it. It does not depend on the video player.

What stays on your Mac

The text is read by macOS, on your Mac. No account, no upload, nothing sent anywhere.
