Open Kare the first time on your Mac

Kare is not notarized by Apple yet. Notarization is part of Apple's developer program, which costs $99 a year, and it is planned. Until then, macOS may refuse to open Kare the first time. It takes four steps, once.

The four steps

  1. Drag Kare into your Applications folder, then double-click it.
  2. macOS shows a message starting with “Apple could not verify…” and does not open Kare. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click Open Anyway next to Kare. Authenticate with your password or Touch ID. If you do not see the button, try to open Kare again, then come back.
  4. Click Open. Kare starts, and macOS remembers your choice.

Why right-click › Open is not enough

Before macOS Sequoia, you could Control-click an app and choose Open to get past this message. Since macOS Sequoia (macOS 15), that is no longer enough: the Open Anyway button in System Settings is the way. Sources: MacRumors, August 6, 2024 and Apple's guide to opening an app from an unidentified developer.

Prefer Terminal? (optional)

Skip this if the steps above worked. Otherwise, this command removes the quarantine flag that macOS puts on downloaded apps, and Kare then opens normally: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Kare.app

Is it safe?

Kare reads the text on your Mac, with macOS's own text recognition, and sends nothing anywhere. Opening an app this way is your decision: do it only for an app you trust, downloaded from its official link.

Questions

Why does macOS say Apple could not verify Kare?

Because Kare is not notarized by Apple yet. It means Apple has not checked this app, not that a problem was found in it.

Do I have to do this every time?

No. Only the first time: macOS remembers your choice.

Will Kare be notarized?

Yes, it is planned. These steps should then no longer be needed.
